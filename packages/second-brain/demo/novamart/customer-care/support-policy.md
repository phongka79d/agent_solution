# NovaMart Customer Care Support Policy

## Service Level and Post-Purchase Commitments

1. **Unopened Return Window (14 Days):** Customers may return any unopened, factory-sealed NovaMart item within **14 calendar days** of delivery for a 100% refund of the paid VND purchase price (`FAQ-1`). Opened items are ineligible for standard return unless covered by a confirmed Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) or warranty hardware defect.
2. **Domestic Shipping Standard (2–4 Business Days):** Orders ship from NovaMart's Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi distribution centres with standard delivery completed within **2–4 business days** nationwide.
3. **Hardware Warranty Coverage (12 Months):** All NovaMart laptops, monitors, accessories, and storage devices carry a **12-month limited hardware warranty** from the date of delivery.

## Order Lookup and Identity Verification Rules

- **Authoritative Status Source:** Order status answers must always reflect NovaMart's live order records, never unverified mirror statuses.
- **Strict Identity Verification:** Private order details may be disclosed **only** after the customer's channel identity is verified and the requested order is confirmed to belong to that exact authenticated customer.
- **Fail-Closed Denial:** Anonymous storefront sessions, unverified identities, or cross-customer sessions must be denied private order information without leaking order contents or status.
