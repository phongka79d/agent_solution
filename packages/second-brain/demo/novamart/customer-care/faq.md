# NovaMart Customer Care Frequently Asked Questions

## FAQ-1: What is your return policy?

NovaMart offers a **14-day unopened return policy** from the confirmed delivery date for all laptops, monitors, accessories, and storage devices purchased in Vietnam. To qualify for a full refund in VND to the original payment method, the item must remain factory-sealed, unopened, unused, in its original NovaMart packaging with intact serial labels, and accompanied by the verified order reference (such as `ORD-DEMO-005`). Opened items cannot be returned for buyer's remorse; however, verified hardware defects discovered after opening are covered under NovaMart's 12-month limited hardware warranty.

## FAQ-2: What are your shipping times and delivery centres?

Standard domestic shipping takes **2–4 business days** across Vietnam. Orders are fulfilled directly from NovaMart's two regional distribution centres in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi depending on destination proximity and live available-to-promise inventory.

## FAQ-3: What hardware warranty does NovaMart provide?

All NovaMart hardware products (`NM-L*` laptops, `NM-M*` monitors, `NM-A*` accessories, and `NM-S*` storage drives) include a **12-month limited hardware warranty** starting from the verified delivery date. Warranty service covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal creative and office use, fulfilled via authorized service centres in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.

## FAQ-4: How can I check my order status or speak with a human agent?

Verified customers can ask for their order status by providing their order reference (for example, `ORD-DEMO-005`), which Customer Care checks against NovaMart's authoritative order records once the customer's channel identity and order ownership are verified. Anonymous sessions may read public FAQ policies but cannot access private order statuses. At any time, you can request *"I want to speak with a person"* or *"I want a human agent"* to be connected to a live NovaMart support operator.
