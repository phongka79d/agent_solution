1. **Creative and Graphic Design Professionals:** Customers seeking laptops with dedicated NVIDIA GeForce RTX graphics (RTX 3050, RTX 4050, RTX 4060, RTX 4070), 16GB to 32GB RAM, fast NVMe storage, and factory-calibrated 4K monitors (`Nova Studio` and `NovaColor` series).
