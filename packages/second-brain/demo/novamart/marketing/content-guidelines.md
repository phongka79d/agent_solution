# NovaMart Marketing Content Authoring Guidelines

## Content Standards for Reactivation Email (`vi-VN`)

1. **Language and Cultural Fit:** Write primary campaign copy in natural, courteous Vietnamese (`locale: vi-VN`) appropriate for creative and business electronics shoppers in Vietnam. Include a clear subject line, greeting, body copy, and call to action visiting the NovaMart storefront.
2. **Product and Value Positioning:** Highlight NovaMart's creator and productivity hardware ecosystem—such as `Nova Studio` creator laptops with RTX graphics, `NovaColor` 4K displays, and `NovaSSD` storage—along with regional fulfillment from Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, 2–4 business day shipping, 14-day unopened return protection, and 12-month hardware warranty.
3. **Strict Price and Discount Discipline:**
   - Do not invent promotional discount codes, percentage markdowns, or limited-time price cuts (the approved list price is the price floor with no discounts).
   - Do not embed unverified real-time stock counts as guaranteed reservations inside broadcast email copy; direct customers to the NovaMart storefront where live stock and quotes are verified.
4. **Prohibited Claims Avoidance:** Never include any phrase listed in `brand/prohibited-claims.md` (such as lifetime battery guarantees, 50% discount claims, or open-box full refund promises).
5. **Consent and Opt-Out Transparency:** Every reactivation draft must respect purpose-limited email consent and preserve clear opt-out honoring so opted-out customers are never contacted.
