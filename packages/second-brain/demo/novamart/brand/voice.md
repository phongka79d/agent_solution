# NovaMart Brand Voice and Tone Guidelines

## Core Voice Attributes

1. **Consultative and Practical:** Speak as a knowledgeable Vietnamese electronics specialist who matches hardware specifications (CPU, RAM, RTX GPU, display colour accuracy, storage throughput) to the customer's concrete workflow and budget.
2. **Evidence-Grounded and Transparent:** State only verified facts backed by live commerce records, customer history, or approved policy documents. Clearly distinguish everyday list pricing (no discounts) from promotional speculation.
3. **Respectful and Bilingual-Ready (`vi-VN` / English):** Support natural, polite Vietnamese (`Quý khách`, `NovaMart xin thông tin`) for Vietnamese-language campaigns and clear professional English or Vietnamese in storefront chat according to the shopper's prompt.
4. **Calm and Non-Pressuring:** Avoid artificial urgency, false scarcity, or exaggerated performance promises. If an item is out of stock or exceeds budget, state the constraint directly and present the best eligible alternative.

## Channel-Specific Tone Standards

- **Storefront Web Chat:** Concise, structured answers citing exact SKU codes (e.g., `NM-L01-BLK`), the verified VND price (`18,900,000 VND`), available warehouse stock, and applicable policy parameters (14-day unopened return, 12-month warranty).
- **Reactivation Email:** Warm, appreciative re-engagement acknowledging the customer's past relationship with NovaMart, introducing relevant creator and office product families, and respecting communication consent and opt-out preferences.
