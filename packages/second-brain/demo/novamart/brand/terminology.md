# NovaMart Approved Brand Terminology

## Brand and Product Naming Standards

- **Retailer Name:** Always write `NovaMart` (single word, capital `N` and `M`) or `NovaMart Vietnam`.
- **Product Line Prefixes:** Use exact canonical family names and SKU identifiers:
  - Laptops: `Nova Studio 14 Creator` (`NM-L01-BLK`, `NM-L01-GRY`), `Nova Studio 16 Creator` (`NM-L02-STD`), `Nova Work 14` (`NM-L03-STD`), `Nova Flex 15` (`NM-L04-BLK`, `NM-L04-SIL`), `Nova Air 13` (`NM-L05-STD`), `Nova Studio 15 Creator` (`NM-L06-STD`), `Nova Pro 16` (`NM-L07-STD`), `Nova Work 15` (`NM-L08-STD`).
  - Monitors: `NovaView 24 FHD` (`NM-M01-BLK`, `NM-M01-WHT`), `NovaView 27 QHD` (`NM-M02-STD`), `NovaColor 27 4K` (`NM-M03-STD`), `NovaWide 34` (`NM-M04-STD`), `NovaView 22` (`NM-M05-STD`), `NovaColor 32` (`NM-M06-STD`).
  - Accessories & Storage: `NovaDock USB-C` (`NM-A01-USBC`, `NM-A01-USBA`), `NovaMouse Pro` (`NM-A02-STD`), `NovaKeyboard` (`NM-A03-STD`), `NovaPen` (`NM-A04-STD`), `NovaBag 15` (`NM-A05-STD`), `NovaCam` (`NM-A06-STD`), `NovaSSD 512` (`NM-S01-STD`), `NovaSSD 1TB` (`NM-S02-STD`), `NovaDrive 2TB` (`NM-S03-STD`), `NovaSD 256` (`NM-S04-STD`).

## Commercial and Policy Terms

- **Currency Format:** Express monetary amounts in Vietnamese Dong as `VND` (for example, `18,900,000 VND` or `18.900.000 VND` in `vi-VN` copy). Never use `TWD` or unverified USD conversions.
- **Fulfillment Centres:** Refer to regional distribution centres as `Ho Chi Minh City` (the southern hub) and `Hanoi` (the northern hub).
- **Service Commitments:** State `14-day unopened return policy` (`đổi trả trong 14 ngày đối với sản phẩm nguyên seal/chưa mở hộp`), `2–4 business days standard shipping` (`giao hàng tiêu chuẩn 2–4 ngày làm việc`), and `12-month limited hardware warranty` (`bảo hành phần cứng chính hãng 12 tháng`).
